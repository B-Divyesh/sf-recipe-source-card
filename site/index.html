Transparent by design

No black box between the page and your card.

Recipe import usually hides the interesting part: what the site provided and what the tool changed. Source Card keeps the handoff visible.

  1. 01

    Read the published recipe

    Open a recipe page and click Capture. The extension reads only its exposed schema.org Recipe JSON-LD—not arbitrary page text.

  2. 02

    Repair it in daylight

    Name, author, yield, times, ingredients and steps open as ordinary labeled fields. Nothing is silently rewritten.

  3. 03

    Take a source-linked card

    Export Markdown or JSON. Both formats include the canonical source URL and capture date—even after you edit.
